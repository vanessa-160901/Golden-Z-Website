What we do

Golden Z runs on five departments. Here's what each one is actually responsible for.

Advocacy & Programs

Plans the events and campaigns Golden Z runs on issues affecting women and girls.

  • Organizes campus talks and workshops
  • Runs advocacy campaigns tied to Zonta's global focus areas
  • Coordinates signature events, including our annual Chartering Ceremony

Partnerships & Outreach

Builds the relationships that let Golden Z work beyond campus.

  • Reaches out to hospitals and clinics across Toronto
  • Connects with U of T faculties, including Nursing and Public Health
  • Liaises with other Zonta clubs on collaboration and sponsorship

Communications

Runs Golden Z's public voice.

  • Manages social media and newsletters
  • Maintains this website
  • Keeps departments in sync with each other

Fundraising

Raises the funds behind Golden Z's work.

  • Plans fundraising events and campaigns
  • Manages sponsorship relationships with Zonta clubs and partners
  • Tracks and reports on funds raised

Built By Her

Produces our podcast featuring women building things in their fields, from the interview to the final edit.
