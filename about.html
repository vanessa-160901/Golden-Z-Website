Our Story

How Golden Z started, and what we're building toward.

Our story

Golden Z was chartered in March 2026 as the University of Toronto's first Zonta International student chapter, co-led by Vanessa Feng and Anna Cai. In our first year, we built out a departmental structure, launched a podcast, and worked through what it actually takes to run a student organization built to last past any one exec team.

We're part of Zonta International, a global service organization founded in 1919 in Buffalo, New York. Today Zonta clubs operate in dozens of countries, working through service and advocacy toward gender equality for women and girls, in support of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 5. Golden Z is our chapter's way of bringing that work onto a Canadian university campus.

The Z is for Zonta. It's also for Gen Z — our generation stepping up to make a difference. — Golden Z

What we're building toward

We want Golden Z to be a place where students don't just learn about gender equity — they practice building the kind of organizations that sustain it. Over the next year or two, that means deeper partnerships with hospitals, clinics, and faculties across Toronto, stronger ties with other Zonta clubs, and infrastructure solid enough to hand off cleanly to the next generation of students who lead it.
