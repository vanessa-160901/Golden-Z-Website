Our Initiatives

A few of the ongoing initiatives Golden Z runs throughout the year.

Advocacy & Policy

Raising awareness, pushing for change, and educating our campus on critical issues affecting women and girls.

Gender Equity Campaigns

We run awareness campaigns on campus tied to Zonta’s global focus areas.

  • Highlight issues like gender-based violence and educational access
  • Engage students through interactive booths and digital advocacy
  • Partner with U of T faculty and student groups

Policy Panels & Speakers

We host professionals and academics to discuss policy, leadership, and equity.

  • Connect students with women leading in their fields
  • Explore the intersection of public policy and gender
  • Provide networking and mentorship opportunities for members

Volunteering & Community Service

Taking direct, hands-on action to support vulnerable populations in the Greater Toronto Area and beyond.

Birthing Kit Drives

We assemble and donate birthing kits to support new mothers in need.

  • Packed with essential newborn and maternal supplies
  • Assembled at group events open to all members
  • Distributed through partner organizations

Care Packages for Local Shelters

We assemble and deliver care packages to shelters across Toronto.

  • Packed with everyday essentials
  • Organized in partnership with local shelters
  • Run a few times a year, with member packing nights beforehand

Bake Sales

Low-key, high-turnout fundraisers held on campus throughout the term.

  • Run by members between classes
  • Proceeds go toward our service projects and scholarships
  • A regular, easy way to get involved without a big time commitment

These are just a few of what we’ve got going — new initiatives come up throughout the year as departments identify new needs. Want to help run the next one?
