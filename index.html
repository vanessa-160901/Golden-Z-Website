We're the University of Toronto's first Zonta International student chapter.

Golden Z brings students together to work on advocacy, community programming, and partnerships that build gender equality into campus life and the city around it.

What we do

Golden Z runs on five departments, each responsible for a different part of how the chapter works — from the events we run to the podcast we produce.

  • Advocacy & Programs

    Plans the events and campaigns Golden Z runs on issues affecting women and girls, from campus talks to city-wide initiatives.

Built By Her

Our podcast features women building things in their fields — in science, policy, business, and beyond. Season 1 opened with guest Riyam Ojaimi, produced by our team: Liv, Imane, and Lou-Anh.

Listen and learn more

Looking ahead

Golden Z is only a year old, but we've already built our departmental structure, launched Built By Her, and made it through our first full round of leadership. Over the next year or two, we want to turn that foundation into real momentum: deeper partnerships with hospitals, clinics, and faculties across Toronto, stronger ties with other Zonta clubs, and a chapter that runs on infrastructure built to outlast any one exec team.
